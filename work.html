Legal Services to Exit

TSG Reporting | COO | 2010-2022

The Challenge

Complex, multi-service professional services business (stenography, legal video, translation, interpreters, conference rooms) serving AM Law 100 firms. The company needed operational infrastructure to scale beyond founder-led sales and move from $12M to a position where acquisition made sense.

What I Built

  • Case management system that systematically identified and won business on both plaintiff and defense sides of disputes — creating competitive advantage in a fragmented market
  • Lead qualification and distribution system that matched high-quality opportunities to sales team capacity, improving win rates and reducing wasted effort
  • Revenue analytics dashboards that identified high-value client segments and service combinations, driving strategic account targeting
  • Calendar and scheduling operations serving 500+ daily requests with sub-10-minute response times — all human-powered, pre-AI automation
  • Department scaling playbooks that maintained service quality through rapid growth, including training programs and best practice standardization
  • Executive team recruiting and development (CRO, Controller) to sustain growth beyond my direct involvement

Results

  • Scaled from $12M to $45M in annual revenue over 8 years
  • Improved gross margin 24% through KPI dashboards, forecasting, and disciplined budgeting
  • Initiated and executed sale to industry leader Esquire Deposition Solutions in 2022
  • Built operations that could scale past my departure
  • Established global service footprint (95% US, 3% Europe, 1% Asia)

PE-Backed Franchise Restoration

ServiceMaster TRS | CEO | 2023-2024

The Challenge

Hired by private equity sponsors to acquire and professionalize a Houston-based restoration business, then build it into a scalable multi-market platform through acquisitions. The business needed unified systems, leadership structure, and operational discipline to support national account growth.

What I Did

  • Led acquisition and integration of a second business in Connecticut, establishing true multi-market platform capability
  • Unified two large loss divisions and four branch teams under shared operating model and leadership structure
  • Introduced tech-enabled workflows, job-level P&Ls, and KPI dashboards to improve transparency and field performance
  • Recruited key leaders and restructured org charts to drive coordination and margin discipline across sites
  • Positioned company for national account growth and future acquisition targets through foundational process improvements

What I Learned

The CEO seat taught me where I operate best. I'm most effective as the systems architect and operational leader — building infrastructure, scaling teams, driving execution. I learned that the strategic positioning and capital allocation work of the CEO role interests me less than the operational leverage work of building things that scale.

Fractional Operating Partner

Sustain CFO | Co-Founder & COO | 2020-Present

The Work

Co-founded consulting firm helping founder-led and PE-backed companies build operational infrastructure. We focus on what actually matters: clear planning, disciplined execution, and systems that hold under pressure.

What I Do

  • Partner with founder-led and PE-backed companies to professionalize operations, align leadership, and improve margin performance
  • Lead org design, cross-functional planning, and systems implementation (EOS, OKRs, dashboards)
  • Implement automation and workflow systems to streamline operations and accelerate execution
  • Collaborate with fractional CFO teams to bring strategic clarity to growth, budgeting, and capital allocation

The Limitation

As a consultant, I have minimal control over outcomes. I can build the systems and recommend the changes, but implementation depends on client follow-through. I prefer being in the operator seat — accountable for results, not just recommendations.
